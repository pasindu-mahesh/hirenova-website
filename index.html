Overview

The problem

Hiring teams screen hundreds of resumes and run interviews by hand. Candidates are reduced to keywords, interview difficulty never changes, and human judgement varies from panel to panel.

Our solution

Smart Recruiter fuses an explainable skill graph, a job-grounded adaptive interview, accent-invariant speech confidence and consent-aware visual behaviour into one competency profile a human can review.

04

Adaptive AI Interview

Kannangara P.M.K

Automated recruitment platforms often present fixed interview questions and score answers only afterwards, so the interview cannot adapt to the candidate. The component studied here selects the difficulty of the next question during the interview from three live signals: the semantic score of the previous answer, a stress level, and a behavioral reliability tier derived from voice and video. It lacked an explicit specification, its verification stopped at the service boundary, and evidence for behavior-derived signals is mixed. This thesis aims to specify the component's behavior, verify it, and state how far the evidence extends.

A design-science approach was followed. The component is a FastAPI service within an eight-service platform that runs on CPU with a locally hosted model server. Its control law is simple and testable: a pure three-level difficulty function and a budgeted branching policy (follow-up, fact-weave, or job-description topic rotation) that keeps topic coverage central. Verification combined an automated test suite with measurements of the behavioral signals.

All 175 automated test scenarios passed, including every combination of answer score, stress level and reliability tier. The visual signal performed better than a simple baseline and ran fast enough for real-time use. the scoring of answers has not been checked against human ratings or stakeholder feedback.

The adaptation mechanism is therefore verified, whereas the validity of its input signals is not. The individual contribution is an adaptive control architecture with an executable specification and a verification record separating what is demonstrated from what is not. Remaining gaps concern concurrency, persistence, validity and test coverage.

Keywords: adaptive interviewing, computerized adaptive testing, semantic answer scoring, multimodal behavioral signals, design science research
