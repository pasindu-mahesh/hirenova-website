2. RESEARCH GAP

No existing tool combines explainable skill-graph inference with an interview whose difficulty adapts to live, accent-invariant audio and consent-aware visual signals.

3. RESEARCH PROBLEM

Hiring teams screen hundreds of resumes and run interviews by hand, while candidate evaluation stays inconsistent and hard to justify.

01

Keyword screening hides real skills

Resumes are matched by keywords, so implied competencies are missed.

02

Interviews never adapt

Fixed question sets ignore each candidate's performance and delivery.

03

Accent biases speech scoring

Confidence is judged on wording and accent rather than the acoustic signal.

04

Behaviour is over-claimed

Gaze and facial movement are misread as deception, producing false positives.

4. RESEARCH OBJECTIVES

Make AI-assisted hiring more reliable, explainable and fair — from résumé evidence through to a human-reviewed decision.
01 Build an explainable 0–10 competency profile from a résumé, public GitHub activity and a skill knowledge graph.
02 Report visual behaviour as observations only — gaze and brief facial movement — and never as deception.
03 Estimate delivery confidence accent-invariantly from the acoustic signal, not transcript wording.
04 Ground questions in the job description and adapt difficulty to performance, stress and reliability.
0–10 competency score
Per-skill explainability
Accent-invariant delivery labels
Human makes the decision
